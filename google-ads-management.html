Services · Google Ads management

Google Ads management for Iowa service businesses.

Help setting up and running Google Search ads for local service businesses in Iowa, with plain monthly reporting and clear, separate billing.

Who it's for

Local service businesses in Iowa that want to be found when nearby customers search on Google: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, remodeling, and other businesses that serve a local area.

It's a good fit if you already run Google Ads and aren't sure they're working, or if you're thinking about starting and want them set up carefully from the beginning.

What's included

Work is scoped to what your business needs and agreed in writing before it starts.

Account audit

A review of your existing Google Ads account, with a written list of what to fix and why.

  • Campaign settings, keywords, and ads
  • Conversion tracking and wasted spend
  • Findings written in plain language

Campaign build

A Search campaign set up in your own Google Ads account, built around the services you offer and the area you serve.

  • Keywords, negative keywords, and ad copy
  • Location targeting for your service area
  • Conversion tracking checked before launch

Monthly management and reporting

Ongoing care for your campaigns, with a plain report each month.

  • Regular review of search terms, budgets, and bids
  • Adjustments to ads and keywords
  • A monthly report: what was spent, what happened, and what changes next

How billing works

Your ad spend and Stetwood's management fee are always kept separate.

  • Ad spend: You pay Google directly. The Google Ads account is in your business's name, your own card is on file, and Google bills you for your ads. Stetwood never puts its own card on your account or resells ad spend.
  • Management fee: Stetwood's fee for the work is separate from your ad spend and is disclosed to you in writing before any work begins.
  • Your account: You own your Google Ads account and its data. Stetwood works in it through a manager-account link that you approve and can remove at any time.

Email to ask about the management fee for your business.

No guarantees

No one can promise how Google Ads will perform, and Stetwood doesn't. There are no guaranteed rankings, ad positions, clicks, calls, leads, or sales. Results depend on your market, budget, competition, and Google's own systems.

What you can count on is careful setup, honest reporting, and straight answers about what is and isn't working.
